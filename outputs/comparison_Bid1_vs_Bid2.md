# Comparison: Bid1 vs Bid2

Bid1 targets a large‑scale K‑12 computing rollout with multiple device types, a multi‑year contract and imaging services, while Bid2 is a small, single‑vendor purchase of Dell laptops and docks for a state office with a short‑term delivery schedule. The due dates, delivery expectations and commercial terms differ markedly, as do the submission methods and required documentation. Overall the two solicitations are unrelated in scope, timing and product mix.
